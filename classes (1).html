What to Expect

Know your class
before you arrive.

💪 Strength

Powerlifting & Barbell

Built around the three main lifts — squat, bench, deadlift. Suitable for beginners to intermediate. Coach teaches, spots, and programs progression over 8-week cycles.

🔥 HIIT

High Intensity Interval

Short, brutal, effective. 45 minutes of circuits, intervals, and compound movements. Maximum calorie burn, minimum time wasted. All fitness levels welcome — modifications provided.

🥊 Boxing

Boxing Fitness

Technique-first boxing for fitness. No sparring, no contact — just you, the bags, and combinations that will make your shoulders burn in the best way. Beginners and experienced welcome.

🏃 Cardio

Spin & Cardio

High-energy spin classes with music that actually goes. Or express cardio sessions designed to push your aerobic base without killing your legs before a leg day.

🧘 Yoga

Yoga — All Styles

Power yoga for athletes who want to move better. Yin yoga for people who need to slow down. Sunday flow for both. No spiritual pressure — just movement and breath.

💆 Recovery

Mobility & Recovery

Run by Marcus, a sports physio who knows the difference between good pain and bad pain. Foam rolling, stretching, breathwork. The session serious athletes skip and then regret.
